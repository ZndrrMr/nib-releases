Nib

A quiet writing surface for your Mac.
Write with Apple Pencil on iPad, or with your finger on iPhone.

Download for Mac

macOS 15 or later · Apple silicon and Intel
Signed, notarized, and includes automatic updates.

iPhone and iPad

Install Nib from your TestFlight invitation. Future builds appear in TestFlight automatically. Requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

Connect your devices

  1. Open the disk image, drag Nib into Applications, and launch it.
  2. Allow local network access and enable Nib in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Open Nib on your iPhone or iPad on the same network, select your Mac, and approve the matching code.
  4. For handwriting recognition, save your Cerebras API key in Nib’s Mac settings. Nib uses Gemma 4.
