Nib privacy

Nib connects your iPhone or iPad directly to a Mac on your local network. Pairing information and preferences remain on your devices. Pointer, touch, and raw Pencil input are not sent to a Nib server.

When you use handwriting recognition, your Mac sends a cropped image of the handwriting to Cerebras for Gemma 4 to recognize. Nib does not send the surrounding Mac document. Your Cerebras API key is stored in your Mac’s Keychain and is never included in the mobile app or sent to the paired device. Cerebras processes requests according to your account and its privacy policy.

The Mac companion checks a GitHub-hosted feed for app updates. GitHub receives ordinary network request information when you check for or download an update. TestFlight is provided by Apple and its beta testing terms and privacy practices apply.

Release builds do not persist handwriting diagnostic traces. You can forget a pairing in Nib’s settings.
